# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: open the
**Security** tab of [ddproxy/tuishou-ting](https://github.com/ddproxy/tuishou-ting)
and use **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue or pull request
for an unfixed vulnerability.

Include the affected version, the impact you observed, and the steps needed to
reproduce the issue. You should receive an acknowledgement within the time
below; we will keep you informed as the report is investigated and fixed.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release receives security fixes. Older releases are not
supported; please upgrade before reporting an issue against them.

| Version | Supported |
| ------- | --------- |
| Latest release | Yes |
| Older releases | No |

## Response times

| Severity | Acknowledgement | First assessment | Target for a fix or workaround |
| -------- | --------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Critical | 1 business day | 2 business days | 7 days |
| High | 2 business days | 5 business days | 30 days |
| Medium | 5 business days | 10 business days | 90 days |
| Low | 10 business days | 20 business days | Best effort |

These are targets, not guarantees; complex reports may take longer. The status
of a report will be updated in the private GitHub thread.
